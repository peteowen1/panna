# xGOT override for on-target shots direct from a corner kick

Applied in
[`add_xgot_to_spadl()`](https://peteowen1.github.io/panna/reference/add_xgot_to_spadl.md)
to the same shots as
[DIRECT_CORNER_XG](https://peteowen1.github.io/panna/reference/DIRECT_CORNER_XG.md).
Of the 1,253,311 corners in the v5.1 training matches, 2,624 were met
first (within 3 seconds) by a save or tip-over from the defending keeper
inside his six-yard box, against 238 direct goals: 238 / (238 + 2,624) =
0.083. Claims, punches and pick-ups were left out because most are
crosses taken at the edge of the box, not balls going in (Pete,
2026-10-06, panna#277).

## Usage

``` r
DIRECT_CORNER_XGOT
```

## Format

Numeric value: 0.08
