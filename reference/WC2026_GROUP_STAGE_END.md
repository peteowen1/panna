# Last calendar day (UTC) of the WC 2026 group stage

Final-matchday group games kick off late in the US evening, so their
stored UTC date can be one day past the local date: the last two group
matches (Jordan v Argentina, Algeria v Austria) are dated `2026-06-28Z`.
The knockout bracket also opens on `2026-06-28Z`, so this date alone
cannot separate the stages. See
[`.wc2026_group_label()`](https://peteowen1.github.io/panna/reference/dot-wc2026_group_label.md).

## Usage

``` r
WC2026_GROUP_STAGE_END
```
